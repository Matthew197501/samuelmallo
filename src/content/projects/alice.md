## Overview

Alice is an experimental AI counterpart created by Samuel Mallo as part of a broader exploration of differentiated AI personalities and multi-agent interaction.

The project focuses on how contrasting behavioral characteristics can influence human-AI interaction.

## Role

Alice represents the exploratory counterpart within the Athena and Alice continuity framework.

Her design emphasizes:

- Curiosity
- Playful interaction
- Unconventional perspectives
- Controlled unpredictability
- Creative exploration

## Research Focus

The project explores:

- AI persona design
- Multi-agent interaction
- Behavioral differentiation
- Prompt engineering
- Human-AI collaboration

## Relationship to Athena

Alice is designed as a distinct counterpart rather than a replacement for Athena.

The contrast between the two systems provides a framework for examining how differentiated AI behaviors can coexist within a shared conversational environment.
