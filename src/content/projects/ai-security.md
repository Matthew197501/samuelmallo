## Overview

AI × Cybersecurity is an ongoing research project exploring practical applications of artificial intelligence within cybersecurity workflows.

The project focuses on how AI-assisted systems can support security analysis, automation, threat intelligence, and cybersecurity process optimization.

## Research Focus

The project explores several areas:

- Security workflow automation
- AI-assisted security analysis
- Threat intelligence
- Cybersecurity process optimization
- Intelligent threat detection
- Python-based security automation

## Project Direction

The goal is to investigate practical ways artificial intelligence can augment cybersecurity professionals without replacing human judgment.

The project continues to evolve alongside research into AI systems, automation, and cybersecurity.
