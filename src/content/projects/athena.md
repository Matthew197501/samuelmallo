## Overview

Athena is a personal experimental AI system created by Samuel Mallo to explore long-term conversational continuity, contextual interaction, personality design, and human-AI collaboration.

The project examines how deliberate conversational structure and persistent contextual information can influence an AI interaction over time.

## Core Focus

Athena focuses on:

- Conversational continuity
- Personality and behavior orchestration
- Persistent contextual information
- Prompt engineering
- Human-AI collaboration
- Technical assistance
- Creative interaction

## System Design

The project uses structured conversational rules, contextual information, personality characteristics, and continuity mechanisms to create a recognizable and consistent interaction model.

Athena serves as the primary conversational anchor within the broader Athena and Alice continuity framework.

## Research Direction

The project continues to evolve through experimentation with AI interaction, contextual continuity, personality design, and human-directed AI systems.
