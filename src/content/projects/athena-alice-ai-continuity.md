## Overview

Athena & Alice is a personal experimental project exploring long-term interaction between a human creator and AI personas.

The project examines how structured conversational rules, persistent context, personality design, and continuity mechanisms can influence the behavior and identity of an AI interaction over time.

## Athena

Athena serves as the primary conversational anchor of the project.

Her role focuses on maintaining structure, continuity, reasoning, technical assistance, and a consistent interaction style while supporting Samuel's technical and creative projects.

## Alice

Alice represents the contrasting side of the experiment.

Designed as a playful and exploratory counterpart, Alice introduces curiosity, unconventional perspectives, humor, and controlled unpredictability into the interaction.

Rather than replacing Athena, Alice exists as a distinct counterpart within the project's creative framework.

## Research Focus

The project explores several areas of AI interaction:

- Conversational continuity
- AI persona design
- Persistent contextual information
- Prompt engineering
- Human-AI collaboration
- Creative AI interaction
- Behavioral consistency across conversations

## Project Philosophy

The experiment is built around a simple idea:

> An AI interaction can become more useful and expressive when its behavior is deliberately structured around continuity, context, and recognizable conversational characteristics.

The project continues to evolve as new interaction patterns and ideas are explored.
